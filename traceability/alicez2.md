## Requirement changes

| ID | Original requirement (P3) | Revised requirement | Why it changed |
|---|---|---|---|
| R3 | After canceling a follow-up, users should be able to undo the cancel and have the exact prompt and any files attached return to the same spot in the queue it was originally at. | Users can undo only the most recent cancellation until the queue reaches and skips that canceled follow-up. Undo restores the exact prompt and attachments at their original position relative to prompts still waiting. An “Undo recent cancel” button appears only while that cancellation is undoable and disappears after undo or when the queue permanently removes the canceled follow-up without sending it. Canceling another prompt makes it the new undo target; undoing or removing that target does not make older cancellations undoable again. | The original requirement did not define how long undo remains available or what happens when the queue advances or multiple prompts are canceled. This revision preserves the prompt, attachments, and queue order while defining a checkable undo deadline and single undo behavior. |
